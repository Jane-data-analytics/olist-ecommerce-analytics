
# 📑 Business Insights & Recommendations

This folder contains the business insights and recommendations derived from the Brazilian E-Commerce (Olist) Analytics project.

## 📊 Report Sections

### 1. Sales Performance
Analysis of sales trends, order volume, and overall payment performance.

### 2. Customer Analysis
Understanding customer purchasing behaviour and repeat purchase patterns.

### 3. Product Performance
Identifying top-performing products and product categories.

### 4. Payment Analysis
Evaluating payment methods and payment distribution.

### 5. Seller Performance
Analyzing seller contribution and order performance.

### 6. Delivery Performance
Understanding delivery timelines and order status patterns.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Monitor monthly sales trends to identify growth opportunities.
- Focus on high-performing product categories.
- Improve delivery operations to reduce delays.
- Analyze customer purchasing behaviour to improve retention.
- Evaluate seller performance to identify improvement opportunities.

## 🎯 Project Outcome

The analysis helps transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights using Excel, MySQL, and Power BI.

## 📝 Note

Detailed findings and numerical insights will be documented based on the actual SQL analysis and Power BI dashboard results.
